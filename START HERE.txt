SCREENINK - PORTABLE WINDOWS EDITION

1. Keep all files in this folder together.
2. Double-click "Start ScreenInk.bat".
3. Press F8 to draw on the screen. Press F8 again to return.

ScreenInk starts in the background. Its icon appears in the system tray
(you may need to open the tray's hidden-icons arrow).
Right-click that icon and choose Exit before unplugging your USB drive.

Copy this whole folder to a USB drive or another Windows 10/11 PC.
No installation, internet connection, or administrator access is normally
needed. If using the ZIP, extract the entire ZIP before launching.
Do not run two copies at once: only one app can register the F8 shortcut.
School or work security policies may block PowerShell; consult your IT team.

Drawings and undo history last only until you exit the application.
Use Save PNG to keep your annotated screenshots permanently.
See README.md for all tools and shortcuts.
